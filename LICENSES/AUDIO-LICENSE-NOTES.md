# Audio License Notes

- UIowa MIS guitar files were investigated as the preferred pack from PLAN-v2.
- The source page states the material is freely available and usable without restrictions, but it does not provide an explicit PD, CC0, or CC-BY grant.
- Because the build brief requires every shipped third-party audio sample to be PD, CC0, or CC-BY, the app ships a self-generated Karplus-Strong pack instead of redistributing UIowa material.
